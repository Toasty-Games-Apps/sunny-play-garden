# Handoff

Little Sprout Park is a free, static, dependency-free browser games site for a young child (the first player is 3½, a pre-reader who is working on letters). Serve the folder on any static host or open `index.html` through a local server. No build step.

## Layout
- `index.html`, `styles.css`: shell, screens, overlays, all shared styles (game-specific styles are appended in sections).
- `js/core.js`: player storage (`SPG.store`, localStorage), synthesized sound effects (`SPG.sfx`, WebAudio, no files), soft music, UI helpers (`SPG.ui.press` = act on touch-down, `SPG.ui.counter`, `SPG.ui.dpr`), and safe mode (`SPG.safe`).
- `js/voice.js`: every spoken line (`LINES`), critter noises (`SOUNDS`), two voice sets (male/female) from tablet recordings (IndexedDB) or files in `audio/voice/<set>/` listed in `audio/voice/manifest.json`, built-in speech as fallback, per-line mute, praise frequency.
- `js/studio.js`: the grown-ups "Voices" screen (record, hear, delete, mute, choose voice, cheering frequency).
- `js/art.js`, `js/art-garden.js`, `js/art-pets.js`: all illustration (the frog avatar has its own `frogHead`: eyes on top with lids that blink; `face(..., { noEyes })`) (faces, scenery, fruit, plants, friends, avatars, bucket, shovel/can/hand, cats/dogs/parachutes, firefighters, safety net). Shared so the look stays consistent. Scene sky/hills are cached in offscreen canvases.
- `js/glyphs.js`: handwriting-style letters as ordered SVG strokes. Draws cards and drives the tracing game.
- `js/app.js`: players, hub (games look like small app icons, a square picture with a short name and no play button, nine to a page on tall phones (3x3), eight on wide phones (4x2) and six on tablets of 600px or more both ways (3x2 landscape, 2x3 portrait, slightly larger icons): `perPage()` in js/app.js must match the grid in styles.css; rotating the phone rebuilds the pages and keeps your place (`hubFirst`) in a swipeable strip with big arrows and dots (the arrows move to the sides on short landscape phones); each game's `icon(c,w,h)` must look good in a square; a card opens on a short tap so a swipe never starts a game; the Pet Shop storefront stays at the bottom; the page is remembered when returning from a game; touching the empty background of the main menu changes the picture behind it, cycling meadow, sunset, beach, snow, space, autumn and candy: `BACKDROPS`, `nextBackdrop()` in js/app.js, saved per player in `bag('hubbg')`, looks in styles.css under `.backdrop[data-bg=...]`; Grown-ups night mode still overrides them), parent gate, grown-ups panel (voices, play-time limit, safe mode, players), game start/stop, pause on overlays/hidden tab.
- `games/color-pictures.js` + `games/color-seasons.js` + `games/color-dinos.js` (Coloring Book picture data: 20 everyday + 30 seasonal + 9 dinosaurs, collection `dinosaurs` with its own stompy music theme `dino`) and `games/color.js` (its engine, editor and gallery).
- `js/scenery.js`: eight scenery themes (meadow, sunset, night, autumn, snow, beach, farm, city) with far-away things and weather; `SPG.scenery.draw/fader`. Fire Rescue, Rain Bucket, Puzzle Pond, Fruit Splash, Grow a Garden and Bunny Band change place as she plays.
- `js/people.js`, `js/people-wear.js`, `games/style.js`: the Style Studio (see below). `people.js` draws human children from a small "look" object; `people-wear.js` holds the wardrobe catalogs (`P.CATS`), hooks and the big nail-salon hands.
- `js/pets.js` (pet and hat art, saved pets, the companion that sits beside the drawing) and `games/petshop.js` (the Pet Shop screen).
- `js/sync.js` + `worker/index.js`: optional encrypted cloud backup (see Saving and backups).
- `games/*.js`: one file per game. `SPG.games.push({ id, name, order, icon(c,w,h), create(host) })`; `create` returns `{ start, pause, resume, resize, destroy }`. Add tints for a new game id in `tints` in `js/app.js`.
- `js/native.js`: the only bridge to the Android app (Capacitor). `SPG.native.isApp` is false on the website; in the app it provides `saveFile` (share sheet instead of a download link), the Android back button (-> `SPG.safe.onBack`) and `apiBase` (full address of the cloud backup). Keep every use of a native feature behind it. `SPG.native.widget.setRest(reached)` tells the Android widget (below) that today's play limit is used up.
- `android-app/` (branch `claude-android-app`): the Android app. `build-web.mjs` copies the game files into `www/` (no service worker, CSP gets the backup address) and Capacitor packs them inside the APK, so it runs from the phone with no website. Same package id `com.toastygames.littlesproutpark`, signing from env vars, `.github/workflows/android.yml` builds the bundle. See `android-app/README.md` for owner steps (secrets, version codes, Play Data safety answers). The website (Cloudflare) stays the source of the game files; the app is a copy made at build time.
- **Android widget** (`android-app/android/.../SproutMiniWidget.java`, `SproutCoverWidget.java`, `MiniGames.java`, `SproutWidgetBridge.java`; layout `res/layout/sprout_mini.xml`, providers `res/xml/sprout_mini*.xml`): a parent-added widget for the home screen and the Samsung Flip cover screen with four tap-only mini games (Fruit Pop, Letters, Counting, Memory pairs) and a leaf badge that opens the app. The picture is drawn on a Canvas and split into a 4 x 4 grid of invisible tap squares; each game answers `tap(tile)` (see `MiniGames`). It has no permissions, no network, no sound and never reads the child's saved data; it rests (moon) when the app reports the daily limit reached. The earlier Codex cover-screen widget (SproutWidget, painting gallery, timer sync) was deleted. The hub also adapts to foldables: `squareOpen()` (near-square inner screen: 9 icons), the under-360 px rules, and `coverTiny()` (both sides 420 px or less, a Flip cover: one big game at a time with side arrows and swipe, Pet Shop along the bottom); `perPage()` must match the grid in styles.css.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: installable, works offline (network first). `_headers`: Cloudflare security headers (CSP, no framing, microphone only for this site).

## Age and difficulty, and boy or girl
`js/level.js` (`SPG.level`): every player has a level, chosen at setup or in Grown-ups > Age and difficulty: an exact age 2 to 7 (each game steps up at its own age, `RULE`, so the mix differs between games) or an age group (Toddler 2-3, Little kid 3-5, Big kid 6-7: every game uses that group's tier). Games ask `SPG.level.tier('rain')` and get 1 (easier), 2 (the game as it always was) or 3 (older kids). Saved on the player as `level`; players with none are Little kid, and the old `settings.fruitAge === 'big'` counts as Big kid. What each tier does is listed in `GAMES` in js/level.js (it is also what Grown-ups shows), so keep that text true when you change a game: Letters/Numbers/Math (choices, ranges), Fruit (speed, balloons), Rain (drops per rainbow, speed), Fire (how many flames; at tier 3 they flare back up), Band (tune length), Shape Builder (how many pieces, plain holes), Puzzle (pieces), Hide (friends per place, radar delay), Garden (water needed), Cook (counted ingredients), Clay (how many tools she gets), Fishing (spots and reeling taps), Caterpillars (pattern length, dots/stripes/sizes, missing balls).
The setup sheet also asks boy or girl (`profile.gender`, optional). Grown-up screens use `SPG.pron.fill('... {her} ... {their} ...')` so text never calls a child "her" by default (no choice = them/their). Text the child hears never uses pronouns.
Hide and Seek: who hides in each place is saved with the place (`pr.kinds`) and never repeats a kind from an earlier place in the same adventure, so friends that tag along are not "replaced" by look-alikes (21 kinds, at most 21 friends per adventure).

## Games
- **Letter Garden** (eight activities, shown as app-icon style tiles like the games page: small illustrated scenes drawn in `menu()` of games/letters.js, label underneath; the last two are **Numbers** (touch each picture to count it, then pick the number) and **Add** (add or take away with pictures and handwriting-style digits; `numbers()`, `math()`, digits in js/glyphs.js, spoken `num/0..20`)): trace, flash cards, find the letter (whole alphabet, capital/lowercase mixed, avoids recent repeats), write her name, sounds (pick the picture that starts with the sound), match (pair big and little letters).
- **Fruit Splash**: swipe or tap, up to five fingers at once, no losing (default), rare golden star, lifetime fruit counter, glowing bonus fruit (5 points + a star, rainbow aura), five themes picked from a palette button (classic, neon, candy, space, ocean; `THEMES`, saved as `bag.theme`). older children (age tier 3, see Age and difficulty) get sly water balloons (`balloon()`); popping one shakes the screen, sprays water and subtracts `BALLOON_COST` (5) fruit points from `bag.total` (never stars).
- **Rain Bucket**: catch drops, rainbow every 8 drops, a meadow of kept flowers, drops counter. Every third rainbow triggers **Raining Cats and Dogs**: a fire truck drives in, two firefighter friends hop out and hold a safety net she drags, pets float down on parachutes, bounce, and walk off calmly (misses land softly too); when done the crew folds the net (`P.spread` 1 -> 0), climbs on the truck and drives off together; saved pets are counted and cats/dogs then visit the garden. The rainbow before that (flowers % 3 === 2) brings a gentle **storm**: three more clouds roll in, soft lightning (a pale flash and a thin bolt) and low thunder (`sfx.zap`, `sfx.thunder`), about nine times as many drops; it lasts ~25 s, then the sun comes out (+1 star). See `startStorm`/`updateStorm` in `games/rain.js`.
- **Grow a Garden**: guided planting steps shown by a step strip: dig three times with the shovel (hole grows), drop in a seed, pat the dirt once, then water with a can she grabs and pours (assisted; or switch to tap mode by tapping the can button again). Blooms unlock seeds (pumpkin 3, carrot 6, lavender 10, rose 15) and a bigger garden (8 blooms). 14 friends to meet (some need rainbows or rescued pets), a friends book, and a "say goodbye" tool to send friends off screen.
- **Fire Rescue** (`games/firerescue.js`; the firefighters never rescue on their own: a pet waits, bouncing, until she taps it, and a finished round stays calm with nothing re-igniting until she taps the green arrow for the next place): three colorful buildings with friendly flames in some windows and on the roof, and a cat or dog in one window each. She drags a finger to spray the hose (bear firefighter with the fire truck at the side) and the flames shrink and puff into steam; when a building is out its pet bounces with a star, a tap (or ~5 s) sends the fox firefighter running with a ladder, the pet climbs down and sits in front of the building. Three rescued = rainbow, confetti, +1 star (+1 star per building while playing). Nothing bad happens: if nobody plays, the flames fade by themselves (`IDLE_FAST`, `FADE_RATE`), the pets are helped down anyway, no stars are given, and the game rests on a calm sunny scene until the next touch. Tuning constants (`WET_RATE`, `DRY_RATE`, `FADE_RATE`) are at the top. The hose is one continuous soft stream (`sfx.hose()`, started while a finger is down, stopped on release) and a flame going out is `sfx.puff()`. After a place is finished, if she was playing, the next place starts by itself after `AUTO_NEXT` (5 s, a ring fills round the arrow); if nobody played it stays calm. Reuses `art.firefighter`, `art.fireTruck`, `art.pet`.
- **Coloring Book** (`games/color.js`): 50 pictures in five collections shown as tabs with a colored mini picture each (Animal friends, Garden, Halloween, Thanksgiving and fall, Christmas); the season that is coming up opens first (`seasonNow`). Every separate shape (each ear, raindrop, wing, star) is its own colorable section. Each collection has its own instrumental music (`THEMES` in `js/core.js`). The child's pet, if she has one, sits beside the page and reacts. It has a gallery and a painting screen. Bucket fill, brush (paint is clipped to the section where the stroke began, and never crosses into a section in front of it), eraser (drag to rub out, tap to clear a section), multi-step undo, start over (asks first, and can itself be undone), 14 colors, Done (golden frame in the gallery, +1 star the first time; finishing is never a lock). A child saves a picture one at a time as a PNG (the app shows its own "Saved!"; in the Android app the button puts it straight into the phone's photo gallery album "Sprout Park" with `SPG.native.saveToGallery` and no share sheet, falling back to the gated share sheet only if that fails; on iPhone/iPad it goes through the grown-up gate). There is no voice at all inside the Coloring Book (`SPG.voice.hushed`) and it has its own gentle music-box music, on by default with its own Grown-ups switch (`settings.colorMusic`).
- **Puzzle Pond** (`games/puzzle.js`; the next puzzle is prepared while she plays (`prepareNext`) and starts instantly when she taps the green arrow after finishing): jigsaw puzzles made from her own Coloring Book pictures (`SPG.coloring.draw` renders a picture with her colors, or a bright sample if unpainted; 80% of the time it picks one she has painted). 2x2, then 3x2, 3x3, 4x3 as she solves them. Drag a piece near its place to click it in; the right place glows while a piece is held; a wrong drop just stays put. Piece shapes are real jigsaw knobs (`edge`, `tracePath`). +1 star per puzzle.
- **Pet Care** (`games/petcare.js`): her active pet (or a visiting bunny) in a cozy room. Drag fruit to its mouth (feed), scrub it with the sponge (bath), or tap the moon (bed; night, lullaby, it walks to bed). A thought bubble shows the icon of what it wants; with the fruit tray open it shows the exact fruit it is asking for (`wantFruit`), that fruit is always one of the three on the tray (no duplicates), the wrong fruit is politely refused (head shake, fruit returns, the right one glows) and a new fruit is asked for after each meal. Dirt is drawn as realistic mud (`drawMud`: wet glossy splotches with dried flecks, splatters and drips that disappear one by one as it is washed). Fur (`furBody`/`furHead` in js/pets.js) is soft shading and tiny curved marks, no spikes; note `art.shade` needs six-digit hex colors, so expand `#fff` first. Needs live in `store.bag('care')` and creep back up gently over real time (never past .85; dirt never past .55, about eight stains, and a new pet starts only a little scruffy: one knot, one burr). Fed + washed + slept = +1 star. Once asleep the pet stays asleep until she wakes it (room goes dark, night light in three colors, stars in the morning). For 5 seconds after she puts it to bed (`BED_LOCK`, `bedLocked()`) nothing can wake it: the bed button shows a ring filling up, other buttons and taps do nothing and say nothing, so the pet can walk over and lie down and the sleep/morning lines cannot be spammed. A Decorate tray adds wallpaper and furniture. Tapping the pet zooms to a close-up for stroking with hand, brush or spray bottle; it purrs (`sfx.purr`, or a recorded `purr/<species>` clip). Fur detail (`detail` option in `SPG.pets.draw`) is used everywhere pets are drawn.
- **Pet Care grooming** (`games/petcare-groom.js`, `SPG.careGroom`; the close-up toolbar has six tools: hand, brush, spray, sponge, towel, back): the pet gets dirty in four ways that need different tools. `bag.grime` = stains (`mud` dark and glossy, `dust` soft grey puffs, `grass` green smears, `jam` sticky purple grape jelly, never red so it cannot look like a hurt) each with an amount `a` that a tool scrubs down where it touches (sponge: mud and dust; soap suds make grass and jam come off, `GRIME_BY` has what each tool does on each kind; the brush flakes dry dust and dry mud; the towel wipes), `bag.knots` = matted fur (brush it out; wet fur combs out about three times faster) and `bag.burrs` = prickly seed heads (brush them off or tap one with the hand). `bag.dirt` is now just the sum of the stains (`G.level`, 0 to 1) so the old need hint still works; time makes `seed()` add stains and `furTick()` adds knots and burrs (smooth-skinned pets get no knots). Positions are fractions of the pet's height from its feet, so they look the same in the room and in the close-up (kept at y -.16 or higher so nothing sits under the toolbar). Sponge = soap suds (`this.suds`, they pop or are rinsed off by the spray, the towel wipes them away) and wets the fur (`this.wet`: darker, bluer, clumped fur points, sheen, droplets and drips; the towel and time dry it). `drawGroomed` draws the pet on a spare canvas and paints stains, wetness, knots, burrs and suds on top with `source-atop` so they stay inside its outline. Everything stains-gone / knot-gone / burr-gone has its own little puff and sound (`groomEvents`); all stains gone = the old 'washed' care, all knots and burrs out = hearts and shine, and the bath in the room (sponge on the bath button) uses the same tool. Test: `tools/playtest/care.mjs` (any size); `SPG.careGame.probe()`.
- **Bunny Band** (`games/band.js`): six friends (bunny drum, bear horn, cat xylophone, fox flute, frog bell, panda piano; `sfx.instrument`). Touch to play. Green button = Copy me (a 2-5 note tune, wrong notes just replay it, every second win = +1 star). Red button = record up to 10 s (16 notes) then it loops; touch again to stop.
- **Shape Builder** (`games/shapes.js`, hub order 9, id `shapes`, replaces the old Choo-Choo Train; state in `store.bag('shapes')`: `built`, `seen`): a workshop table with a blueprint-paper board and a wooden tray. A picture (`PICS`: house, rocket, car, sailboat, flower, fish, snowman, castle, tree, kite, ice cream, cat, robot; shuffled, never repeating until all have been seen) is built from coloured shapes (`s` = circle, oval, square, rect, triangle, trapezoid, diamond, semicircle, star in a 100 x 100 design box: centre `x, y`, size `w, h`, turn `r` degrees, colour `c`, youngest tier that must place it `l`, `g` = lights up in the celebration; the array order is the drawing order). Every part is a HOLE showing its exact shape: the hole and the piece are drawn by the same `shapePath(c, s, w, h)` at the same turn, so a piece truly fits its hole. Pieces wait in the tray at one common scale (never tiny, never bigger than their cell) and grow to exact hole size while held (lifted above the finger). A piece fits only a hole with its own shape, size, turn and colour (`keyOf`); two identical holes (the two wheels) take either piece. Dropping it on a hole it does not fit, or anywhere else, slides it home (a soft boing only when it was on a wrong hole); after two wrong drops its own hole pulses, and after 9 s of nothing a hole pulses and its piece wiggles. While a piece is held every hole it fits glows. Tiers (`SPG.level.tier('shapes')`, RULE [3, 5]): 1 = the 3 or 4 `l: 1` parts to place (the rest of the picture is already built) with coloured holes, 2 = also the `l: 2` parts (5 to 7 pieces, coloured holes), 3 = every part (8 to 11 pieces), plain shadow holes, a small preview of the finished picture at the top for the colours, and two look-alike decoys (right shape, a colour the picture does not use for that shape) in the tray. Snapping speaks `shape/<name>` (and `color/<name>` at tier 1). A finished picture celebrates (`CHEER` motions in `MOVES`: the house lights its windows and puffs smoke, the rocket launches with flames, the car drives off, the boat sails, the fish swims with bubbles, the snowman hops in snowfall, the castle shoots fireworks, ...), +1 star, then the next picture slides in. Night mode (`SPG.night.on()`) gives a dark workshop. To add a picture: add an entry to `PICS` (keep every piece inside the box, `l: 1` parts 3 to 4, 1+2 parts 5 to 7, all parts 8 to 11, and no two parts with the same shape and colour but different size or turn unless that is intended); `SPG.shapesGame.probe()` and `loadPic(id)` are for tests (`tools/playtest/shapes.mjs` plays every picture at every tier by dragging).
- **Hide and Seek** (`games/hide.js`, rebuilt as a permanent world): a square map she walks around in (drag/tap to walk). Five places in a chain (`MAPS`: meadow, farm, village, woods, beach; 3-5 friends each) whose layout is seeded and never changes; only the season (from the date: snow, leaves, blossoms) changes how it looks. A glowing trail leads to something special (a well, windmill, fountain, magic tree, lighthouse, sign: `LAND`), houses (`HOUSE`) line the way, and the edge exit is locked until every friend there is found; then a gate and a trail point to the next place. Found friends tag along as followers across places (`FRIENDS`, a follower "snake"). Guidance: a radar arrow/glow that grows more solid when she is close (warm/hot), and ears peek out after a while. The yellow circle that used to appear around a spot was the hot/cold glow when she stood near a hider; it is now the radar. All found in the last place = "Everyone is home" and a star. Layout test: `hide_*.js` build notes are in the git log of this branch.
- **Style Studio** (`games/style.js`, hub page 1): dress-up for eight preset children (four girls, four boys, all skin tones) with 11 picture tabs: who, hair (22 styles + salon tools comb/dryer/sparkle spray/bubble wash), make-up (lips, eyeshadow, blush, freckles, face gems), princess dresses (ball gown, party, tutu, mermaid, flower fairy, sundress), tops, skirts/pants, shoes (glass slippers...), crowns and hats, extras (glasses, necklaces, fairy/butterfly/angel wings, capes, wands, balloons...), nail salon (two big hands, 16 polishes, glitter, nail art decals, drag across nails) and places (backdrops). A look is a plain object (`P.defaultLook`); each child keeps their own in `store.bag('style')`. Every tab and item name is spoken (`style/<key>` lines in `STYLE_NAMES` in js/voice.js). The dice button dresses her randomly; the rosette button sends her down a runway (+1 star when the look changed since the last show). To add clothes: add a draw function to the matching table in `people-wear.js` and an entry in `P.CATS`, then add its spoken name to `STYLE_NAMES`. About 100 items in all (more are added by the `add(...)` blocks in people-wear.js). Note `art.shade` only takes hex colors; the people files have their own `shade` that also takes `rgb()`.
- **Hair Salon** (Style Studio > Hair tab, the scissors tile at the top of the hair list; `games/salon.js`, strand engine in `js/hair.js`, `SPG.hair`): eight messy hairstyles are dealt each time (`H.generate(kind)` from `KINDS`: longTangle, longWavy, shaggyBob, wildCurls, shortCurly, messyPony, unevenPigs, spikyMop, choppyFringe, bedhead, a random eight of the ten, random colours, the dice button deals again; each kind is a recipe of locks with uneven lengths, knots, a crooked fringe and stray hairs). She picks one and it opens big in a mirror with a barber cape (`drawEdit`: hair behind the body, the figure with `hair: 'none'`, the cape, then the hair in front). Hair is a list of strands (root, heading, length, curl, gravity, messiness `ms`, wetness `w`, optional tie point `vx, vy` for ponytails and pigtails, knots `kn`, flyaway `fly`); the path is rebuilt from those numbers (`path()`, cached per strand by `s.v`), strands in a lock fray apart while messy and lie together when brushed. Tools: **scissors** (kid scissors with round tips; a snip every ~110 ms along her swipe, a tap snips a short line; `H.cut` shortens every strand the blades cross to that point with a blunt end and the loose ends fall away as pieces), **brush** (`H.brush`: smooths messiness, pulls knots out one by one with sparkles, flattens stray hairs; wet hair brushes out about three times faster), **spray** (`H.spray`: wets the strands near the nozzle, they get darker, glossier, heavier and hang straighter), **dryer** (`H.dry` dries them and the hair flutters). Undo keeps 14 steps; three stars at the top fill as the hair gets tidier (`H.neat`: messiness, knots, stray hairs, and some trimmed ends); the green check saves it (`store.bag('style').cuts`, newest 6, packed with `H.pack`, +1 star and praise if it is tidier than a quarter and she changed something) and she wears it right away: `look.hair = 'cut:<id>'`, `P.cuts[id]` holds the unpacked hair and `people.js` draws it through `style.back/front` (so the hair colour palette still works; the style functions now get the hair colour as a 4th argument). Saved cuts appear after the scissors tile ('my hairstyle'); the oldest is dropped past 6 and anyone wearing it goes back to 'long'. Test: `tools/playtest/salon.mjs` (any size); `SPG.salon.cur.probe()`.
- **Pet Shop** (`games/petshop.js`, art and saving in `js/pets.js`): a storefront on the hub (not a game card: `shop: true` in its registration). Inside it is a store: an awning, sign tabs (Pets / Hats / Glasses / Bows and collars), wooden shelves with glass-fronted pens (cage, basket, pond, ice, perch, pasture, forest, cloud: `penKind`, `drawPen`) and a pegboard for accessories, plus a mirror-and-podium dress-up corner with a wardrobe row per body place. Stars buy a pet (16 species: bunny, kitten, bear, fox, frog, panda, puppy, hamster, duckling, piglet, lamb, mouse, penguin, owl, elephant, unicorn; 10-40 stars) and accessories (5-20). Accessories have a `slot` (head, face or neck) and a pet can wear one of each at once (`owned[id] = { name, hat, face, neck }`; `P.wear(itemId)`, `P.unwear(slot)`; every game that draws the pet passes `hat`, `face` and `neck`). To add a species: an avatar head in `art.avatar` (js/art.js), an entry in `SPECIES` and `PETS` (js/pets.js) and a pen in `penKind`; add its name to `PICK_NAMES` if you add names (js/voice.js). every purchase asks first; in the accessory sections (pegboard) the first button is "None" (take it off), then what she owns (touch to wear, no price), then what she can still buy; an accessory can only be on one pet at a time: one that another pet wears shows that pet's face and cannot be put on (`P.wornBy`, old saves are de-duplicated on load); a pet gets a name (dice button picks one), one pet at a time is the companion (beside the drawing in the Coloring Book and walking along the front of the bed in Grow a Garden, cheering on every bloom; tap it for its noise). No real-money purchases exist anywhere. **Names are picked, not typed**: a grid of name buttons (`SPG.ui.nameGrid`) says each name out loud when touched, and the dice picks one; the keyboard field only appears after the grown-up maths gate (`SPG.app.askGate`). Player setup works the same way (a grid of nicknames, plus a gated 'Grown-up: type a name' button). Prices live in `PETS` and `HATS`.
- **Dinosaurs** (added with the dino round): 9 dinosaur coloring pages (`games/color-dinos.js`, group `dinosaurs`, music theme `dino` in `THEMES`); five dinosaur pets in the Pet Shop (baby dino 20, triceratops 30, stegosaurus 30, brontosaurus 35, T. rex 40 stars; species flags `smooth`, `dino`, `plates`, `tall` in js/pets.js, heads in `art.avatar`, a `jungle` pen in `penKind`/`drawPen`); Pet Care uses them like any pet. **Rain Bucket meteor shower** (the bucket becomes a glowing star net, `art.catcher`, for the whole event) (`startMeteors`, `updateMeteors`, `drawMeteors` in games/rain.js): queued when `flowers % 4 === 1` (never overlapping other events); 3-4 dinosaurs stand on the bottom, rocks fall in columns away from them, the bucket catches rocks, rocks that land become soft glowing stones, dinosaurs always run away from anything near them; +1 star at the end and a transition card each way.
- **Fish Tank** (`games/aquarium.js`, hub order 11, state in `store.bag('aquarium')`): touch the water to drop food (pellets in the water at once = "more food" level), fish swim to it and eat, grow small -> medium -> big (`NEED`, meals count double/triple with better food), grown fish that are not hungry (`HUNGRY_AFTER`) drop coins (silver/gold, rare pearl worth 10); touch a coin to collect it (coins wait on the sand forever). ## Tuning notes
Letter Garden's `later(fn, ms)` holds a step that comes due while the game is paused (screen off, an overlay) and runs it on resume, so a quiz never freezes half way; use it (not a bare `setTimeout`) for anything that moves a question on.
Tracing tolerances are constants at the top of the Tracer section in `games/letters.js` (`TOLERANCE`, `START_RADIUS`, `LOOKAHEAD`); progress follows the nearest path point and cannot outrun the finger. Watering effort is `NEED_DROPS` and the drop rate (`cn.emit`) in `games/garden.js`. Kid-facing buttons use `SPG.ui.press` (touch-down); anything needing a completed tap (fullscreen, first sound) stays on `click`.

## Coloring Book: how it works and how to add pictures
- What a child has done is saved per player (`store.bag('color')`) as data, not pixels: an ordered list of operations per picture in the picture's own 1000 x 800 coordinates (see the comment at the top of `games/color.js`). Undo removes the last operation and redraws. The list is compacted on save (anything a later fill of the same section covers is dropped). Colors are saved as positions in `PALETTE`, so only ever append to that list.
- Rendering uses two stacked canvases: paint underneath, outlines and fixed details on top. Sections are Path2D shapes listed back to front; each paint operation is clipped to its section minus the sections in front of it, so shapes may overlap freely as long as no single section crosses itself.
- **Add a picture:** append an object to `PICTURES` in `games/color-pictures.js` (everyday) or `P1`-`P3` style lists in `games/color-seasons.js` (set `group` to a collection id from `SPG.pictureGroups`; to add a collection, add an entry there with a `theme` for its music) (`{ id, name, sections: [{ id, d, det? }] }`, sections back to front, each `d` a closed path ending in `Z`, all inside 1000 x 800; the helper functions at the top build ellipses, blobs, leaves and so on). Then run `node tools/check-pictures.mjs`: it fails on duplicate ids, unclosed paths, self-crossing shapes and shapes too small to tap (44 units), and warns on small shapes or 6-15 section guidance. Never rename or delete a section id that has shipped, or children's saved colors for it are lost. The gallery, hub card and saving pick the picture up automatically.
- Downloads: a hidden link starts a normal file download and the app shows its own "Saved!". A web page cannot switch off the browser's or Android's own download notice.

## Safe mode (what it is and is not)
A website cannot lock a device. Safe mode = fullscreen (or installed PWA), no external links anywhere, CSP that blocks any outside load, back button stays in the app, fullscreen-lost overlay, and every way out is behind the parent gate (spelled-out addition). The real lock is Android screen pinning, explained in the grown-ups panel.

## Grown-ups panel features

'Forgot the PIN?' offers two hard sums in a row that clear it, so nobody is locked out. ## Voice
**The short list and Extra audio help:** a grown-up is only asked to record **100 lines** (`CORE` in js/voice.js: 5 cheers, 26 letter names, 26 letter sounds, numbers 1 to 10, about 33 short instructions (one per game) and a greeting per player); `voice.allKeys()` is that list and `voice.moreKeys()` is everything else (about 550 optional lines, shown under "More lines" in Grown-ups > Voices and in RECORDING.md part 2). Lines that are not on the short list are **silent by default** (`isExtra()`) unless they are instructions in `INSTRUCT` (what to do next, each said once at its moment: kitchen steps, garden steps, the math question...; always spoken because she cannot read) or in `SPOKEN` (letter/, sound/, word/, num/, color/, shape/, name/, pname/, player/, fish/: they are said by a recording or the built-in voice) or **Extra audio help** (`settings.audioHelp`, Grown-ups > Sound, off by default) is on, which also speaks names of things touched, extra hints and cheers and speaks a little slower. A new instruction line is silent by default; add it to `INSTRUCT` if she needs it to know what to do (and say it once, never on every touch), and to `CORE` only if it really is one of the ~100 that matter (keep the total at 100 or fewer). **Music steps back while a line is spoken** (`SPG.music.duck(on)`: a gain stage under the music bus, used by `speak` and `playBuffer` in js/voice.js), and at night the built-in voice speaks at volume .7 (`speechVol`). **Pronunciation fixes** for the built-in voice live in `SAY_AS` (js/voice.js): a lone "Ay" was read as "eye", so `letter/a` is spoken as "A." (the text shown for recording is unchanged, and edited or recorded lines are never overridden). Add other mispronounced lines there.
Grown-ups > Voices: any number of extra named voices (grandparents, family, her own: `voice.addVoice/renameVoice/removeVoice`, saved in `settings.voices`, clips stored as `<voiceId>/<key>`), a mix switch per voice (`settings.voiceOff`), a guided recorder that shows one line at a time with a progress bar filling as the person speaks, moves on by itself, can be paused and can switch to another person at any time (`renderGuide` in js/studio.js), and any line can be reworded (`settings.lineText`, `voice.setText`; `voice.textFor` gives the current wording). Names in the pickers are real lines (`name/<Name>` from `PICK_NAMES`) and so are the player's own name (`pname/<id>`); nothing the games say should be a free-text `{say: ...}` any more, except a typed name.
**First-run voices prompt:** the first time the Grown-ups panel is opened (after the gate, so a new player goes straight from setup to the games; only if `SPG.config.recorder`; `voiceIntro()` in js/app.js), grown-ups get a full-screen "Welcome, grown-ups!" Test scripts that open the Grown-ups panel must dismiss it (click `#vintro .btn.quiet`).
**Cloud backup includes voices:** with a family code, the named voices (`settings.voices`, deletions in `settings.voicesRemoved`) and every recording are encrypted with the family key and uploaded one file per line under scrambled names (`/api/voice`, keyed hash of `clip:<voice>/<line>`); newer recording wins by stamp (`spg.vstamps` in localStorage), nothing is replaced by an older one; deleting a voice removes it everywhere, turning backup off deletes the cloud copy (js/sync.js `runVoices`, worker `vput`...). Joining a family code on a second device downloads the voices too. **Android app voice:** inside the Capacitor app the built-in voice is the phone's own text-to-speech through `@capacitor-community/text-to-speech` (`nativeTTS()` in js/voice.js; the manifest declares the `TTS_SERVICE` query so Android 11+ can see the engine), including the picker; on the website it is the browser's speech synthesis. **Built-in speaking voice:** lines nobody has recorded use the device voice; `pickVoice()` scores voices (en-US, neural/network/enhanced preferred online, compact/robotic avoided, no big pitch bump) and Grown-ups > Voices lists the built-in voices as buttons grouped Female / Male / More, most natural first, robotic ones left out (`voice.ttsChoices()`, `ttsPicker` in js/studio.js); touching one chooses it and plays a sample (`settings.ttsVoice`); the phone's own voices are used, and a note tells grown-ups how to install better Google or Samsung English voices.

## Saving and backups
- Data lives in the browser's `localStorage` (`spg.v1`, per device; not cookies, no server). `SPG.store` stamps the active player on every save (`t`); device settings (play time, night mode, PIN, hub order, choosing a player) use `store.saveSettings()`, which does not stamp, so an idle device never looks newer. Merges copy into the existing objects (`adopt` in js/core.js) so a game that is open keeps writing into the real save; only a grown-up's restore or joining a family code keeps an undo copy (`mergeProfiles(remote, { undo })`), and a reset stamps `resetAt` so an older copy cannot bring pets or pictures back. Leaving the game screen any way always destroys the running game (`show` -> `stopGame` in js/app.js), asks the browser for persistent storage, flags a failed save (`store.saveFailed`, shown in Grown-ups), keeps removed/reset players in `store.trash` (Grown-ups > Bring back), and keeps one undo copy before any restore (`store.snapshot`, `undoRestore`).
- `js/sync.js` + `worker/index.js` + `wrangler.jsonc`: optional encrypted cloud backup on Cloudflare (Durable Object, no KV/IDs to set up). Family code -> id + AES-GCM key, so the server cannot read anything. Merge rules are in `mergeProfile` (`js/core.js`): newer player wins, coloring pictures merge per picture, owned pets/hats are unioned; deletions never propagate. Backup file save/restore is in the same Grown-ups section. Full notes and deploy steps: `store/CLOUD-SETUP.md`. The service worker never caches `/api/`. Keep play relaxed and unlimited, no losing, no reading required, big touch targets. Don't repeat game mechanics across games. Add new games through the `SPG.games` list.

## Icing and the pet-name sheet (latest)
Kitchen icing helpers live in games/cook-art.js: `drawFrostSwirl` (cupcake swirl on the muffin), `drawCookieIcing` (glossy sparkly fill clipped to the cookie), `drawPiped` (piped lines), `glitterField`/`twinkle` (sparkle animated from `performance.now()`). ## Latest round (reordering, device unlock, kitchen layering, peeking)
- **Rearranging the hub** (js/app.js, `enterHubEdit`/`startHubDrag`, CSS at the end of styles.css): hold an app about two seconds (it swells), the grown-up gate appears, then the apps jiggle and can be dragged onto each other's place; holding a dragged app at the screen edge turns the page; Done / Original order sit in a bar at the bottom. The order is `settings.hubOrder` (game ids; games not listed come after, in their normal order) and applies to every player on the device. Cards ignore taps while editing.
- **Fingerprint / phone lock for the grown-up gate** (`SPG.native.deviceAuth` in js/native.js; button `#gate-device` in the gate sheet; switch in Grown-ups, `settings.deviceAuth`): in the app the phone's biometric prompt with the screen lock as fallback (`@capgo/capacitor-native-biometric`, added to android-app/package.json, needs `npm install` + `cap sync` in the build), on the website WebAuthn's platform authenticator. It is an extra way in: the PIN or sum still always works, and it is not offered when a new PIN is being typed or for the recovery sums.
- **Sprout Kitchen:** everything she adds to a food (icing lines, sprinkles, toppings, sauces, salt) carries a sequence number (`g.zn()`, `z`) and `drawPiece` draws them in that order, so later things go on top; the icing pen also draws on the plate (`kind: 'pen'` in `g.paint.plate`, `PEN_R`); several cupcakes each get their own mini plate (`g.mini()`, `drawPlates`); the camera button rests `CAM_COOL` (8 s) after each photo (grey with a ring that fills back up).
- **Shape Builder** replaced the Choo-Choo Train (see Games).
- **Hide and Seek:** a hiding friend peeks out beside its place every ~2-3.5 s while she is within about 3.6 units (`peekP`, `peekT`), with a tiny squeak from the nearest one.

## Style Studio and Letter Garden updates (latest)
- **Choices pop out**: in Who and Make-up the skin, eye, lip, eyeshadow and cheek choices are compact tiles (`section()` in games/style.js, `.st-chip`); one touch opens a pop-out picker (`openPicker`/`renderPicker`, `.st-modal`) that stays open while she tries colours and closes with the X or a touch outside. Choosing a colour is silent (no more "Eye color!" on every touch): `swatchRow` never speaks. Lips have a **rainbow** (`P.LIPS`).
- **Hands**: the small hands on the characters (`W.miniHand` in js/people-wear.js: palm, four fingers, thumb, polish following each finger) are drawn last in `P.draw`, in front of every hairstyle and hat. The big nail-salon hands (`P.drawHand`) have tapered fingers, a thumb, knuckle creases and tendons; each nail's position, size and angle come from its finger (`FING`, `nailGeom`) so polish always lines up; `nailDraw` draws a natural almond nail with gloss, cuticle shadow and, unpainted, a white edge and half-moon. Snow dress sleeves follow the arm; the royal dress trim stays inside the skirt.
- **Practice lines** (Letter Garden > Trace, switch at the top of the letter picker, `bag.traceMode` = 'one' | 'lines'; class `Practice` in games/letters.js): pick a letter and get a lined page (top line, dashed middle line, baseline) of that letter in capital and small, side by side, over and over. She traces them in order (or touches any one to start there); finishing the page gives +1 star and a fresh page of the same letter; the arrows change letter and the round button starts a new page. One-by-one mode (it moves on by itself) is unchanged.
